0Session Configuration Layer — set by God Mode / Facilitator before play

Seven independent levers are chosen at session creation. They multiply into a very large space of distinct playthroughs before a single player decision is made. Players never see most of these settings.

🎛️ Paradigm 5

legacy_abc · advanced_climate · un_sdg · healthcare · multi_toggles. Each swaps the round-config provider (paradigm_registry.py). Only multi_toggles adds the 5-pillar layer.

🏭 Vertical BU swaps 5

Oil & Gas, Banking/FS, Retail/FMCG, Agriculture, Technology substitute default slots under slot-fit rules. Each carries its own R1 "blindspot" flag.

⚙️ Difficulty tier 3

Easy (0.5× prob / 0.7× impact), Standard (1.0×/1.0×), Expert (1.5×/1.3×). Also sets the treasury floor and adaptive crisis scaling.

🌍 Region 5

ASEAN · South Asia · Europe · North America · Africa — unlocks region-specific Black Swan events.

🏁 Ending pathway 5 + random

Replaces the R10 crisis. Chosen explicitly or random. Drives R5–R8 foreshadowing.

🧩 Side tracks 0–6

Facilitator assigns any subset of 6 parallel mini-sims + injection timing. Some have prerequisites (a DAG).

⏭️ Extended horizon opt-in

Adds Rounds 11–20 (Green-bond maturity → 5-yr final valuation) when activated.

🔧 Turnaround mode opt-in

A 4-stage crisis-recovery ladder (crisis → stabilisation → recovery → exit) with capped CapEx and M_R.

2Side Tracks — 6 Parallel Mini-Simulations

Self-contained tracks (4–10 rounds each) that pause the main sim when injected. They read main state in (seed_from_main_state) and write flags/modifiers back out (write_back_to_main). Some require another track first — a real dependency graph.

🔗 Supply Chain

7 rounds · window R3–R8. Visibility, supplier risk, circularity → cuts Black-Swan probability. Enriches the Ethics track.

prereq: none

⚖️ Ethics & Sustainability

5 rounds · window R2–R7. AI governance, modern slavery, greenwashing, biodiversity.

prereq: supply_chain

🤝 Stakeholder Management

4 rounds · window R1–R6. Salience mapping, IR, community licence, crisis comms → SLO floor + fatigue modifiers.

prereq: none

📊 Sustainability Reporting

5 rounds · window R2–R8. CSRD/ESRS, TCFD/ISSB, assurance → regulatory_readiness.

prereq: ethics_sustainability

🌐 Corporate SDG Deep Track

5 rounds · window R1–R8. PAI → living wage → circular procurement → biodiversity → integrated reporting. Feeds M_SDG (0.97–1.26).

prereq: none

🇮🇳 BRSR NGRBC Deep Dive

10 rounds · window R2–R8. All 9 NGRBC principles, Essential vs Leadership, BRSR Core assurance → brsr_net_positive_dividend (+0.05 M_R).

prereq: sustainability_reporting + ethics_sustainability
Prerequisite chain: supply_chain → ethics_sustainability → sustainability_reporting → brsr_ngrbc. Stakeholder Management and Corporate SDG stand alone. Each track's write-back flags can raise (or lower) the main-sim M_R and starting positions of downstream tracks.

3The R5 Branch — Strategic Archetype Classification

At the R5 checkpoint classify_player_archetype() reads cumulative reputation, social licence, carbon intensity and trend, then routes the player into one of four archetypes. This sets a crisis-severity multiplier and reskins the R6–R10 themes. Adaptive scaling then layers on top: strong performers get harder crises (up to 1.3×), strugglers get eased ones (down to 0.8×).

easier crises (earned) standard harder — proving pivot hardest — the reckoning

🌱 Regenerative Leader

rep ≥65, SLO ≥60, CI ≤45. Crisis ×0.8, +10 stakeholder support. R6–R10 = scaling & legacy opportunities.

⚖️ Pragmatic Optimizer

rep 40–65, treasury ≥$30M. Crisis ×1.0. The "sensible middle" — disruption tests.

🔄 Turnaround Candidate

rep improving vs R2 + recent ESG spend. Crisis ×1.2, +5 support. R6–R10 = credibility tests.

🔥 Fragile Extractor

rep ≤40, SLO ≤40. Crisis ×1.5, −10 support. R6–R10 = "the reckoning," survival mode.

Shadow Board (also at R5): reject Planet director → planet_expendable (−0.20 M_R, amplifies Climate Black Swan) · reject Shareholder → shareholder_alienated (−0.20 M_R in Hostile Takeover) · reject Governance → governance_fragility (−0.25 M_R in Regulatory Shutdown + BRSR leak).

Each pathway also carries 4 archetype-override skins that rename the final outcome (e.g. Climate → Climate Pioneer 🌍 / Adapted Enterprise 🛡️ / Stranded Giant 🏭 / Fossil Relic 🦴), selected by the terminal Regenerative Multiple. 5Terminal Valuation → Company Archetype (the real "ending")

After R10, the whole game collapses into one number — the Regenerative Multiple (M_R, 0.0–~2.03) — built from every flag earned across all layers, then multiplied by exit multiple, SDG multiplier (side-track), and pathway difficulty. M_R lands the company in one of five terminal archetypes.

M_R ≥ 1.8🌱 The Regenerative Titan — ESG leadership converts to premium enterprise value.
1.2 – 1.79🏦 The De-risked Safe-Haven — resilient, defensible, mid-premium.
0.8 – 1.19⚠️ The Fragile Giant — large but exposed; instability discount bites.
< 0.8💀 The Stranded Relic — value destroyed by deferred ESG risk.
Survival mode🔧 The Turnaround Manager — M_R capped at 0.80; the 4-stage recovery ladder.
M_R composition: base +1.00, then bonuses (materiality +0.10, synergy +0.15, resilience +0.20, ethical AI +0.15, community +0.18×JT, just transition +0.12×JT, workforce +0.10, wellbeing +0.05, BRSR dividend +0.05) minus penalties (planet-expendable −0.20, instability discount −0.40). M_SDG (from the Corporate SDG side track only) multiplies the whole valuation 0.97–1.26.

6Always-On Overlays (stochastic, every round)

🦢 Black Swans

8 global + 5 region-specific events roll each round (evaluate_black_swans). Probability compounds when metrics are poor. Difficulty tier scales prob & impact.

🎲 Scripted stochastics

R5 cyclone damage roll (>0.75 → $12M, cut by resilience), R9 strike probability, greenwashing engine, macro interest-rate cycles, FX risk.

🔮 Foreshadowing

R5–R8 news items keyed to the chosen ending pathway. Players see the hints, never the pathway name.
