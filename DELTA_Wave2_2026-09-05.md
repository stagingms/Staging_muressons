## WP-23 — FIN-09 fine cap, IMP-05 swan flows, targeted swans activated

Three separate causes, each verified by attribution (the fine amounts and the swan rolls
were captured in both the old and the new code on the golden game):

1. **FIN-09 — regulator fine capped.** The golden game reaches regulator *enforcement*

2. **IMP-05 — a black swan's revenue/OPEX percentages are flows for the event's

   R8–R10 OPEX stayed ~$7M/round higher and R10 EBITDA was $42.7M. After: the base is
   restored at tick end and the flow is re-applied for the continuing round only; R10
   EBITDA is $56.4M. This is what moves `historical_ebitda`, `tco2e_emissions` (emissions
   scale with the revenue base) and the BU rows from R8, and the terminal valuation.
3. **Targeted swan effects now apply (new finding, WP-23).** `impacts_applied` never
   carried `revenue_pct_reduction_targeted` / `opex_pct_increase_targeted`, so
   `apply_black_swan_impacts` read 0 for them: the embargo ("Electronics and Pharma BUs
   face 15% revenue loss and 10% OPEX increase") and every other targeted event were
   inert. They now cost what they say: the embargo's R7 flow is $10,019,140.14 for each
   of two rounds on the golden game. This is the R7 treasury movement being +9.9M rather
   than the +14.8M of the fine alone, and the R8/R9 net −0.66M / −0.64M.

## C-1 — the greenwash bar is the team's share of the CSF pool (owner calibration ruling, 2026-09-05)

One cause. `calc_greenwashing_risk` compared the AVERAGE of the per-BU investment ratios
(each BU's CapEx over the same pool) with the 15 % / ≈10 % bars, and the absolute escape was
the average CapEx per BU against $3M. The same 15 % therefore meant 60 % of the pool for a
four-BU group and 15 % for a single-BU company, and the archetypal real team — one BU funded
at ~50 % of the pool, $1 in the other three (169 of the 241 stored BU rows are $1) — was a
greenwasher on every full claim (average 12.5 %). A green claim is a group decision; it is
now backed by the group's spend: the team's pool share (Σ per-BU ratios = Σ CapEx / pool)
against the same bars, and total CapEx against the same $3M floor. Per-BU neglect keeps its
own consequence — the natural-decay tier on each BU's own ratio (F-17, WP-21). The other
readers of the average ratio (synergy, floors, regulatory-floor erosion ≥ 0.20, operational
layer) are unchanged.

**Goldens: unmoved.** Both golden games back their claims either way (financial average 0.6,
stakeholder average 0.3125). **Runner fingerprints (`test_treasury_waterfall`): 8 of 15
re-pinned.** The runner gives every BU 10 % of the pool, so a four-BU team put 40 % of the
pool behind each claim and was still penalised from R4 (average 10 % < 15 % / 10.05 %).
